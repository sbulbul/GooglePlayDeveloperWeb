Reminder Hub

One place for credit card due dates, subscriptions, bills, loans, insurance, medicine and your own custom reminders.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Credit card statement and payment due-date reminders, with optional last-four-digit card labels.
  • Subscription, bill, loan and insurance tracking with weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly cycles.
  • Medicine schedules with dosage, time and prescription dates.
  • Custom reminder groups for anything else, such as home maintenance.
  • Timeline, calendar view and a monthly expense summary.
  • Home-screen widget.
  • Pro (optional annual subscription): English voice input and import from your device calendar.

Privacy first

Your reminders are stored on your device. Reminder Hub has no account sign-up, no ads and no tracking SDKs, and does not send your records to our servers.
